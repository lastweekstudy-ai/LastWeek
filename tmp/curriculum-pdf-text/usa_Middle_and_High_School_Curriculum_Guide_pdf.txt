Middle School and High School Curriculum Guide Middle School (Grades 6–8 | Ages 11–14) The curriculum bridges elementary and high school by broadening content knowledge and preparing students for sequential academic tracks. Core Subjects • English: Reading comprehension, literature analysis, writing, grammar, vocabulary, research skills. • Mathematics: Arithmetic review, ratios, proportions, introductory algebra, pre-algebra, and often Algebra 1. • Science: General Science, Life Science, Physical Science, scientific inquiry, experiments, and lab skills. • Social Studies/History: Geography, ancient civilizations, world history, civics, and cultural studies. Electives • Foreign Languages: Introductory language learning, vocabulary, conversation, and culture. • Band/Choir: Music performance, theory, rhythm, and ensemble participation. • Computer Science: Digital literacy, coding basics, problem-solving, and technology skills. • Health: Nutrition, wellness, personal development, and healthy lifestyle choices.

High School (Grades 9–12 | Ages 14–18) High school operates on a credit-based system where students take mandatory subjects and electives to earn a diploma. English (4 Years) • Literature • Composition • American Literature • World Literature • Research and Analytical Writing Mathematics (3–4 Years) • Algebra 1 • Geometry • Algebra 2 • Pre-Calculus • Calculus Science (3–4 Years) • Biology • Chemistry • Physics • Environmental Science • Laboratory Investigations and Scientific Research Social Studies (3–4 Years) • World History • United States History • Economics • United States Government / Civics Other Graduation Requirements • World Languages (typically 2 years) • Fine/Performing Arts (1–2 years) • Physical Education and Health • Computer Science
