• Unit 1: Core concepts and introductory skills. • Unit 2: Applied learning, analysis, and project work. • Unit 3: Research, problem solving, and communication. • Unit 4: Advanced topics and interdisciplinary connections. Learning Objectives: • Demonstrate mastery of grade-level standards. • Develop critical thinking and analytical reasoning. • Strengthen communication, collaboration, and research skills. • Apply knowledge to real-world situations and project-based learning. Assessments: Quizzes, unit tests, presentations, essays, laboratory activities, projects, and cumulative examinations. Skills Developed: Reading, writing, quantitative reasoning, scientific inquiry, digital literacy, creativity, teamwork, and independent learning.

Subject Progression Overview English Language Arts Progression Grade 6–8: Reading comprehension, grammar, literary analysis, argumentative writing, research projects. Grade 9–12: Composition, rhetoric, American literature, world literature, advanced research, public speaking. Mathematics Progression Grade 6–8: Ratios, equations, geometry, statistics, pre n algebra, algebra readiness. Grade 9–12: Algebra I, Geometry, Algebra II, Trigonometry, Pre n Calculus, Calculus, Statistics. Science Progression Life Science → Earth Science → Physical Science → Biology → Chemistry → Physics → Environmental Science. Social Studies Progression Ancient Civilizations → World Geography → World History → US History → Government → Economics → Civics.
